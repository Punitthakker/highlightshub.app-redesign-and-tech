Questions

Which camera do I need?

Anything that shows the whole pitch. 1080p minimum, 2.7K or 4K recommended. A phone on a tripod behind the goal line works.

Do my mates need the app?

No. Anyone with the link can watch, share the story card and send the GIF to the chat without installing or signing up.

We filmed in two parts. Problem?

Upload the parts in order and we treat them as one match. Multi-game nights work the same way.

What if no goals are found?

You get told straight away, with tips on the camera angle. A 0-0 still gets a game page with the reel.
